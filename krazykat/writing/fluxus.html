Fluxus

certain circles
circumscribed
in fleeting curve
*
construction
formed as
like joins like
attraction
*
graceful structure
shaping
empty spaces
adding weight to
this surrounding
point of veiw
advantage
or perhaps a
veiwing process
*
diagrammed
*
defining links
into and outward
*
points
exist within
a value rule of
quickness
likeness
and direction
opacity having
both negative
and positive
affection
*
internal shifting
recursive waves
unnoticed
concealed in every
action
*
pressure
attempted measure
gradual motion
of motives
feelings
specific and
abstracted
modes of reason
*
i breathe
a deep examination
*
touch
travelled heads
in revolution
*
acceptance
spun
self and unself
sprung into one
*
even as i catch
glimpse
flattened scurry
in perfect
unfitting line
of myself
*
hiding
*
shaking
steadily
*
is it role
not goal?
pushing
and what if
what for
*
closure
desperate enclosure
closer
*
intermixed
and scattered
pauses
left waiting
aftertaste
as i swing
*
described
in arc
over us
*
over
*
over
*
over now.
