Scream At Flat Water

maddening impulse
- rolling -
restrained impact
into laughter
broken white lines
leading to
cul-de-sac
gasoline friction empty spaces
howling across
blackened surface
- burning -
reflections of
electric light
over clipped lawns
water beading
on cut blades keratin vibration
- pulsing -
through still air
in suspension
unbroken flat
tone - stretching
the trigger of
that
maddening laughter - speedballing - children tumble
knotted -
over clipped lawns
soaking dew
embedded blade
cutting stone
breaking surface
- watching -
flat water
break
