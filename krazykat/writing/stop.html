Stopping

remembering distracted bliss
flipping to fear as you screamed 'stop!', drops of your sweat pooling
in omega tatoo.
freeze, shudder, lost breathing.

in brittle silence
i thought i'd hurt you inside. and you gripped me harder,
wrapping me inside out
to lean-lick smiling relief
at the nape of my neck.
"stop, be still..." my shoulders unknotted
in your fingers, fertile musk rising up
between our joined bodies, as you came.

*

remembering, stopping, to let you pass through.
