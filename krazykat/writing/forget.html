Forgetting

Dumb slow, flown through.
Syrup. Molasses. Honey.
Got to where you don't know.
The calm security,
that shouldn't
be in your body,
is there.
You've watched yourself
get into this state
many times before,
but never smiled at your mistakes
quite as happily as now.
