Until 3 - Recompiled (Tension)

Exhiliration stretching into dread,
Hard lines cut by your own hand.
You taught it; break it's hold.

Waiting, nervous waiting, teasing
With glimpses of apathetic angels.
Your dreams come hard inside you.

Fearing it's unrecognized shapes,
Lusting for some sort of opening
To shed light and amaze you.

Until it's soft push turns you,
Your tight smile whining in pity
Folding back into your pinched face.
