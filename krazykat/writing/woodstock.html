Woodstock

Somewhere i read,
No. Everywhere.
However subtly put,
the idea that one should live
entirely in the present.

peripheral movement
nailshine trail
of open giving
palms
turned and
snatched back
I've tried, and tried,
felt sure that to
really know every second
as it happens, must be the point.
That elusive meaning.

thick smoke rope tight
round blind eye
smoke cry
under eye
under
lying me
But understanding,
realization and discovery
aren't constant states of mind.
Sometimes a rush gives only painful
loss in passing.
follow dotted
wiggle bobbing yellow
tiny frantic wings
beating
beating
crazy bird
makes me want to laugh.
