Until 1 - Original

The tension of possibilities,
stretching your dread
and exhiliration,
until the lines snap
taught, and break.

The nervousness of waiting,
teasing your cynicism
and weightless ideal,
until your dreams come
hard, inside you.

The cowardice of depression,
suffocating your wonder
and feeding on self,
until fattened it turns
away, smiling wanly.
